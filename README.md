# Things To Do In Eorzea

Things To Do In Eorzea is more than just a simple to-do-list. It's one that automatically monitors what you need to collect, what duties you need to clear, FATEs and more! You can either set it up to track items you need or have it track your clears. Think that's not enough? You can even set yourself up some ridiculous tasks for yourself like doing MINE Bahamut or a whole Savage round without dying/wiping to just simply forcing yourself to play a specific job, or speedrun. There are always things to do in Eorzea. Just get creative!

Unlike other to-do plugins, Things To Do In Eorzea:
1. Auto-tracks your completed dungeons, trials and raids.
2. Auto-tracks your completed FATEs by region, a specific FATE or any FATE in general.
3. Auto-tracks the items in your inventory, making sure you have what you needed to get.
4. Make self-imposed challenges like doing Minstrel's Ballads on MINE difficulty, speedrun a Dungeon, one-shot/no kill, and more.

And you still have your sticky note for whenever you need it.
